## The story so far

I started in banking analytics at ICICI, where I partnered with **8 stakeholder groups** to deliver dashboards tracking **15+ KPIs** and analyzed data for **$8M+ initiatives** covering **300K+ accounts**. Process improvements cut defect resolution time by **10%**, saving roughly **200 analyst hours per quarter**. After earning my M.S. in Business Analytics as a Dean Excellence Scholar at UT Dallas, I joined FleetPride as a Digital Analyst, analyzing **8M+ monthly events** and leading **4 complex investigations** across **5 data sources**. Performance trends drove a **15% revenue improvement** worth **$900K annually** across **3 product verticals**.

Today at JPMorgan Chase, I work on consumer banking product lines processing **$2B+ in annual spend**. I delivered revenue and demand analysis for the Mobile Stealth Mode launch covering **1.2M active customers** and forecast a **$4M incremental impact**. I led **4 cross-functional projects** automating reporting, resolved **7 critical reporting gaps** through root cause analysis, cut data prep time by **35%**, and surfaced **$1.8M in cost savings**. I identified and executed **5 efficiency initiatives** improving the financial outlook by an estimated **$3.2M** across Consumer, Product, and Operations, and built models for headcount planning covering **80+ FTEs** with pricing recommendations adopted by Finance leadership.
